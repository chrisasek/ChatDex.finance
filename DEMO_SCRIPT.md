# ChatDex demo script

## Headline journey

1. Open WhatsApp and send `How is NVDA doing?`.
2. Show the compact demo quote and tap **Buy**.
3. Enter `$50` in the encrypted WhatsApp Flow.
4. Open **Review & Buy** in ChatDex.
5. Continue in demo mode, review the refreshed quote and confirm the paper order.
6. Show the immutable-looking receipt and reference.
7. Open `/app` and show the same position and receipt in Chat and Home.
8. Send `How's my portfolio?` from the linked demo number and show the privacy-aware summary.

## Claims to make

- Information and preparation stay in WhatsApp.
- Execution requires authenticated ChatDex confirmation.
- The stock execution is explicitly Demo/Paper.
- WhatsApp, Chat and Home share PostgreSQL state.
- Duplicate delivery and confirmation paths are idempotent.
